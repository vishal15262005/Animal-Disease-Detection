Claim Process

What to Do if Your Animal Dies

Follow these 5 steps to file a claim and receive your compensation as quickly as possible.

1

Inform the Insurance Company

Contact your insurer immediately after the death of the animal. Do not delay.

⏰ Must be done within 24 hours
2

Contact a Veterinarian

Call a registered veterinarian to visit and examine the animal's body for cause-of-death verification.

3

Obtain the Death Certificate

The veterinarian will issue an official death certificate specifying the cause of death. This is a mandatory document.

4

Submit All Insurance Documents

Gather and submit your death certificate, original policy document, Aadhaar, and bank details to the insurer.

5

Receive Compensation

After verification, the compensation amount is credited directly to your bank account via DBT transfer.
